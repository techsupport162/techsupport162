### 🧭 About Us

The Starhorse IT Department helps keep people, systems, and services running smoothly. We support day-to-day technology operations, maintain reliable infrastructure, and build practical automation to reduce repetitive work and improve service quality.

- 🛠️ Supporting users, endpoints, and core business systems
- ⚙️ Automating routine IT tasks with scripts and tooling
- 🔐 Maintaining secure, stable, and efficient environments
- 📈 Improving service delivery through monitoring and process optimization
- 🤝 Collaborating on tools that make IT operations easier and faster
